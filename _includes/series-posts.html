{% comment %} Assigns `series_posts`: every post in a series, in reading order. The series is page.series, or `series=` when included from a page listing several. Order: posts that declare `series_part: ` are sorted by that number, so a series reads correctly no matter what publish dates its parts carry. Any post without the field falls back to date order, which keeps every other series on this site behaving exactly as before. {% endcomment %} {% assign series_name = include.series | default: page.series %} {% assign series_keyed = site.posts | where: "series", series_name | where_exp: "p", "p.series_part" | sort: "series_part" %} {% assign series_dated = site.posts | where: "series", series_name | where_exp: "p", "p.series_part == nil" | sort: "date" %} {% assign series_posts = series_keyed | concat: series_dated %}
